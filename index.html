Build your readiness kit

Everything in reach when every second matters.

Our pet first aid kit is thoughtfully assembled for everyday accidents and unexpected emergencies. Add one to your course registration, or purchase it on its own soon.

  • Pet-safe essentials
  • Simple, labeled organization
  • Course-friendly quick reference guide
What’s inside the first aid kit View full contents

A thoughtfully selected set of 22 types of essential supplies for common pet-care emergencies.

Care and relief

  • 1 instant cold compress
  • 1 emergency blanket
  • 3 triple antibiotic ointment packets
  • 5 alcohol prep pads
  • 3 sting relief pads
  • 2 diphenhydramine tablets
  • 1 0.5 oz. saline eye wash and skin rinse
  • 1 1 oz. bottle of hydrogen peroxide

Wound care

  • 5 adhesive bandages
  • 5 antiseptic wipes
  • 3 sterile 3 in. × 3 in. wound pads
  • 2 rolls of elastic bandage wrap
  • 2 rolls of vet wrap
  • 2 triangle bandages with safety pins
  • 1 roll of adhesive tape
  • 2 pairs of vinyl gloves

Tools and extras

  • 1 styptic pencil
  • 1 5 mL oral syringe
  • 10 cotton-tipped applicators
  • 1 wood tongue compressor
  • 1 pair of tick-removal tweezers
  • 1 pair of scissors

Contents may vary slightly based on availability. Use supplies according to their instructions and contact a veterinarian when appropriate.
